  0x001C: DUTY_CYCLE — read/write, 8-bit PWM duty
  0x0020: FREQ_TUNE — read/write, 16-bit signed frequency adjust
  0x0024-0x00FF: Reserved

Processes (2):
  1. Write channel FSM — AW → W → B handshake
  2. Read channel FSM — AR → R handshake
