1.0  SYSTEM OVERVIEW & DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
================================================================================

The Advanced MHD Propulsion Module replaces all mechanical propulsion components
(propellers, shafts, gearboxes, turbines) with a non-cavitating, non-magnetic,
thermally-managed Magnetohydrodynamic thruster. The design philosophy follows the
Ghost Limb metabolic rhythm principle: full functionality retained across all tiers,
with metabolic efficiency varying by operational load.

DESIGN AUTHORITY: POG2 Sovereign Systems
DOCUMENT CONTROL: Master Technical Specification
REVISION: 1.0
CLASSIFICATION: Engineering Archive — Build Reference

PHYSICAL FOOTPRINT:
- Thruster active area: 1.0 m² (5 chordwise segments × 0.1 m each, 1.0 m span)
- Total system mass (dry): ~11,500 kg (2.3% of 500-ton vessel displacement)
- Dry ice magazine: 10,000 kg (60 cartridges, 0.3m × 1.5m, aerogel insulated)
- Salt hydrate bellows: 279 kg (encapsulated, elastomeric)

OPERATIONAL ENVELOPE:
- Idle: 0 knots, <20 dB acoustic, zero magnetic signature
- Stealth creep: 2–5 knots, 35–45 dB, transpiration active
- Transit sprint: 15 knots, 45–55 dB, transpiration inactive
- Thermal limit: 320 K (T_CRIT_LIMIT)
- Limp mode trigger: 47 ticks (30.1 seconds) in CRIT state

2.0  FLUID DYNAMICS & ELECTROMAGNETIC FOUNDATIONS
================================================================================

2.1 GOVERNING EQUATIONS

The Lorentz force drives propulsion:
    F = J × B

Where:
    J = Current density (A/m²)
    B = Magnetic field strength (Tesla)
    F = Thrust force per unit volume (N/m³)

For the linear channel:
    τ = J · B · L_channel
    P_dissipated = (J² / σ) · V_channel

Where σ = 5 S/m (seawater conductivity at 25°C, 35 ppt salinity).

2.2 TARGET THRUST DENSITY

Target: 10,000 N/m² for 100-ton vessel with 10 m² total thruster array.

MAGNET TECHNOLOGY COMPARISON:

| Technology        | B_gap (T) | J_required (A/m²) | P_dissipated (MW) | Shaft power @ 50% η (MW) |
|-------------------|-----------|-------------------|-------------------|--------------------------|
| HTS (cryogenic)   | 5.0       | 1,000             | 1.0               | 2.0                      |
| Halbach NdFeB     | 2.3       | 2,174             | 4.7               | 9.4                      |

Selected: Halbach NdFeB permanent magnet array at 2.3 T.
Rationale: Eliminates cryogenic infrastructure, mechanical noise, and LN₂ logistics.
Trade-off: 4.7× more electrical power, manageable with 5,000 V DC architecture.

2.3 BOUNDARY LAYER PHYSICS

At 15 knots (7.72 m/s):
    Re_L = 6.43 × 10⁶ (turbulent)
    C_f = 0.0592 · Re_L^(-0.2) = 0.00257
    τ_w = ½ · ρ · U² · C_f = 78.5 Pa
    u* = √(τ_w / ρ) = 0.277 m/s

At 5 knots (2.57 m/s):
    u* = 0.09 m/s
    τ_w = 8.2 Pa

Gas void fraction requirement for 50% coverage:
    α_w = q_g / (q_g + u*)
    For α_w = 0.5: q_g = u*

CO₂ mass flux at 5 knots:
    ρ_g (194 K, 1.3 atm) = 3.59 kg/m³
    ṁ" = ρ_g · q_g = 3.59 × 0.09 = 0.32 kg/(m²·s)

MISSION MASS PENALTY ANALYSIS:
- 15 knots, continuous, 5 m²: 0.995 × 5 × 3600 = 17,910 kg/hour (UNSUSTAINABLE)
- 5 knots, 1 m² active: 0.32 × 1 × 21600 = 6,912 kg/6-hour stealth (MANAGEABLE)
- 24-hour mixed mission: ~10,000 kg total (2% displacement)

2.4 HYDRODYNAMIC PRESSURE DISTRIBUTION

External seawater pressure varies along chord:
    P_ext(x) = P_∞ + ½ · ρ · U² · C_p(x)

Flat-plate turbulent boundary layer pressure drop:
    dP_ext/dx = -τ_w / δ(x)
    δ(x) = 0.37x / Re_x^0.2

At x = 0.25 m (mid-chord):
    Re_x = 1.6 × 10⁶
    δ = 0.0052 m
    dP_ext/dx ≈ -15,000 Pa/m
    Total chordwise ΔP ≈ 7.5 Pa (NEGLIGIBLE vs. capillary pressure)

3.0  ELECTRODE ARCHITECTURE & TRANSPIRATION PHYSICS
================================================================================

3.1 SEGMENTED CHORDWISE DESIGN

The 1.0 m² electrode is divided into 5 chordwise segments (0.1 m each), each with
2 spanwise halves (left/right). Total: 10 electrically independent sub-electrodes.

SEGMENT PRESSURE SETPOINTS (Stealth Mode, 5 knots):

| Segment | Position   | Choke Setpoint (atm) | Target α_w | Active Area |
|---------|------------|----------------------|------------|-------------|
| 1       | 0–0.1 m    | 1.35                 | 0.70       | 0.2 m²      |
| 2       | 0.1–0.2 m  | 1.30                 | 0.55       | 0.2 m²      |
| 3       | 0.2–0.3 m  | 1.25                 | 0.45       | 0.2 m²      |
| 4       | 0.3–0.4 m  | 1.20                 | 0.35       | 0.2 m²      |
| 5       | 0.4–0.5 m  | 1.15                 | 0.25       | 0.2 m²      |

This creates a graded gas blanket: thickest at the leading edge where wall shear
is maximum, tapering aft. Total CO₂ mass flow is conserved but actively shaped.

3.2 RETICULATED FOAM ELECTRODE

Material: Reaction-bonded porous Silicon Carbide (SiC), doped with TiO₂
- Porosity: ε = 0.60 (60%)
- Pore size: 20 ppi (pores per inch), ~1,000 μm nominal
- Thickness: 15 mm
- Permeability: K = 1.5 × 10⁻⁸ m²
- Forchheimer coefficient: β = 450 m⁻¹
- Span: 1.0 m
- Chord: 0.5 m (5 segments × 0.1 m)

3.3 CHOKE MEMBRANE

Material: 99.8% high-density sintered Alumina (Al₂O₃)
- Thickness: 1.0 mm
- Permeability: K ≤ 1.0 × 10⁻¹¹ m²
- Pressure drop: ΔP = 0.15 atm = 15,000 Pa
- Role: FLUIDIC FLYWHEEL — dominates over foam capillary variation (288 Pa) by 50×,
  forcing uniform gas distribution despite foam heterogeneity.

3.4 CAPILLARY ENTRY PHYSICS

Capillary pressure for bubble emergence:
    ΔP_c = 2 · γ · cos(θ) / r_pore

Where:
    γ = 0.072 N/m (seawater/CO₂ surface tension)
    θ = 0° (non-wetting, cos(θ) = 1)
    r_pore = 500 × 10⁻⁶ m (1 mm pore radius)

    ΔP_c = 2 × 0.072 × 1 / (500 × 10⁻⁶) = 288 Pa ≈ 0.0028 atm

3.5 DARCY-FORCHHEIMER FLOW VERIFICATION

Gas phase pressure field through foam:
    ∇P_g = -(μ_g/K) · v_g - β · ρ_g · |v_g| · v_g

At segment 1 (leading edge, P_net = 0.246 atm = 24,900 Pa):
    ΔP/L = 24,900 / 0.015 = 1.66 × 10⁶ Pa/m
    1.66×10⁶ = (1.01×10⁻⁵ / 1.5×10⁻⁸) · v + 450 · 3.59 · v²
    1.66×10⁶ = 673 · v + 1616 · v²
    v = 31.7 m/s (superficial velocity inside foam)
    v_interstitial = v/ε = 52.8 m/s

Emerging gas flux:
    ṁ" = ρ_g · v · ε = 3.59 × 31.7 × 0.6 = 68.3 kg/(m²·s)

This is 213× higher than the 0.32 kg/(m²·s) surface requirement, confirming that
the CHOKE MEMBRANE is the rate-limiting step, not the foam. The choke homogenizes
flow despite ±20% pore size variation in the foam.

3.6 TAPERED MANIFOLD TOPOLOGY

The plenum chamber uses passive geometric pressure regulation via variable cross-
sectional area and fluidic friction (Blasius correlation for turbulent duct flow).

COMPUTED MANIFOLD HEIGHTS (1.0 m spanwise width):

|---------|-------------|----------------|-------------------|
| 1       | 40.00       | 1.35           | Inlet boundary    |
| 2       | 28.34       | 1.30           | Step-down         |
| 3       | 19.12       | 1.25           | Step-down         |
| 4       | 11.45       | 1.20           | Step-down         |
| 5       | 5.10        | 1.15           | Trailing boundary |

Governing: Compressible 1D continuity + Blasius friction loop.
Mass flow total: 0.32 kg/s for low-speed stealth profile.

3.7 LIMP MODE CHECKERBOARD SHEDDING

In thermal overrun (state 111001), the system sheds segments 2 and 4 completely.
However, to preserve spanwise thrust symmetry, the physical implementation uses a
CHECKERBOARD left/right pattern:

- Pattern A: Segments 1L, 2R, 3L, 4R, 5L active (5 sub-electrodes)
- Pattern B: Segments 1R, 2L, 3R, 4L, 5R active (5 sub-electrodes)

Current reduction: 10,870 A → 5,435 A (50%)
Thrust retention: 75% of baseline (due to non-linear hydrodynamic pressure
distribution — leading edge contributes disproportionately)

This is a BETTER limp mode than originally designed: the vessel retains 75% thrust
while halving power consumption and thermal load.

4.2 TIER 1: SALT HYDRATE BELLOWS

Material: Glauber's salt (Na₂SO₄·10H₂O) with gelation and nucleation additives
- Base salt: Na₂SO₄·10H₂O (phase change at 32.4°C = 305.4 K)
- Gelling agent: 6% cross-linked polyacrylamide hydrogel (5–7% cross-link density)
  - Prevents incongruent melting and phase separation
  - Resists osmotic shock from hyper-saline pockets
- Nucleating promoter: 5% sodium tetraborate decahydrate (borax)
  - Provides crystalline nucleation sites
  - Prevents supercooling, ensures rapid rehydration
- Encapsulation: FEP (fluorinated ethylene propylene) outer skin + silicone rubber
  inner bellows layer
- Geometry: Corrugated flexible tubes (bellows-style), accordion expansion along
  single axis
- Volume compliance: 30–50% expansion/contraction cycle
- Enthalpy: 254 kJ/kg
- Thermal capacity: 279 kg × 254 kJ/kg = 70,866 kJ
- Spike protection: 118 kW × 600 s = 70,800 kJ (10-minute full-load protection)

PASSIVATION CHEMISTRY:
During idle/purge, the salt hydrate releases distilled water of hydration (139 kg
total pure water reserve). This water can:
1. Rinse chloride residues from electrodes during purge
2. Maintain 30–40% RH in CO₂ plenum (prevent static discharge)
3. Serve as emergency fresh water reserve (139 liters)

4.3 TIER 2: DRY ICE PLENUM

Material: Solid CO₂ (dry ice)
- Phase change: Sublimation at 194.7 K (-78.5°C) at 1 atm
- Enthalpy: 571 kJ/kg
- Density: 1,560 kg/m³
- Storage: 60 cylindrical cartridges, 0.3 m diameter × 1.5 m length
- Insulation: 50 mm aerogel blanket (λ = 0.015 W/m·K)
- Heat leak: ~2 kW total (negligible vs. 118 kW active load)
- Plenum pressure: 1.3 ± 0.05 atm
- Thermal lag: τ = x² / (2α) = (0.015)² / (2 × 1.2×10⁻⁴) = 0.9375 s
  (SiC backing plate thermal diffusivity α = 1.2×10⁻⁴ m²/s)

SUBLIMATION CONTROL:
- Resistive heating elements embedded in dry ice pellet bed
- Electronic throttle control (not ambient heat leak)
- Proportional-Integral control: Kp = 1.2, Ki = 0.4
- Anti-windup clamping: ±0.5 atm·s
- Heater output drives sublimation rate directly

4.4 TIER 3: SEAWATER BASELINE

Unlimited capacity convective heat rejection through hull.
Activated when dry ice is depleted or during extended low-power operation.

4.5 THERMAL-MECHANICAL INTERFACE (CTE MISMATCH MITIGATION)

Boundary: Alumina choke membrane (Al₂O₃) + Silicon Carbide foam (SiC)

Material properties:
- Al₂O₃: E₁ = 370 GPa, ν₁ = 0.22, α₁ = 8.1×10⁻⁶ /K
- SiC:   E₂ = 410 GPa, ν₂ = 0.14, α₂ = 4.0×10⁻⁶ /K

Differential thermal strain (ΔT = -110 K, rapid cooldown):
    Δε = (α₁ - α₂) · ΔT = (8.1×10⁻⁶ - 4.0×10⁻⁶) · (-110) = -4.51×10⁻⁴

Maximum unmitigated shear stress (rigid planar boundary):
    G_avg ≈ 160 GPa
    τ_max = G_avg · Δε = 160×10⁹ × (-4.51×10⁻⁴) = -72.16 MPa

With chevron interlocking track relief (2.4× surface area multiplier):
    τ_eff = 72.16 / 2.4 = 30.06 MPa

Safety factor vs. sintered ceramic joint limit (45 MPa):
    SF = 45 / 30.06 = 1.50 (33% safety margin)

Interface design:
- Material: Co-fired micro-porous Zirconia-toughened Alumina (ZTA) intermesh matrix
- Geometry: Micro-machined interlocking chevron tracks
- Function: Distributes expansion stresses across 3D surface area
- Porosity gradient: Dense matching boundary at choke plate → open structure
  matching SiC foam pore pattern
- Acts as structural strain relief sleeve, maintaining fluid seal across thermal cycles

4.6 ELECTRICAL ISOLATION

High-current bus bar (10,870 A) must be thermally coupled but electrically isolated
from the CO₂ manifold.

- Dielectric shield: 3.0 mm sintered high-purity Al₂O₃
  - Dielectric breakdown: 15 kV/mm × 3.0 mm = 45 kV
  - Thermal conductivity: 30 W/(m·K)
- Thermal coupling: Diamond-infused phenyl silicone fluid paste
  - Thermal conductivity: >12 W/(m·K)
  - Completely non-conductive
- Mounting: Ceramic standoffs (Al₂O₃) with thermal grease interface

- Outer shield: Braided ground mesh wire, connected to main hull ground

5.2 SiC-MOSFET SEGMENTED SWITCHING

Per-segment switch matrix:
- Devices: 19 parallel Silicon Carbide MOSFETs
- Example device: Wolfspeed C3M0032120K (1,200 V / 120 A)
- 3 series strings for 3.6 kV derating from 1.2 kV device rating
- Total per segment: 19 parallel × 3 series = 57 devices
- All 5 segments: 285 total devices

Gate drive specifications:
- Optical isolation: Prevents EMI coupling to control substrate
- Linear shutdown ramp: 8.2 ms ± 0.1 ms
  - Prevents transient magnetic pulses detectable by MAD
  - Dampens inductive kick (E = ½LI² = 0.177 J at 5,000 V / 1,880 A)
- Desaturation detection: < 2.2 μs fault response

CONTACTOR ALTERNATIVE (discarded for production):

6.3 GHOSTSPLAT THERMAL PREDICTOR

Forward-looking thermodynamic estimator. Evaluates current inputs and projects
the system's thermal slope 3 ticks into the future (t + 1.92 s).

Taylor-series expansion with thermal diffusivity:
    T_(t+3) = T_0 + 3Δt · (α·∇²T_0 / Δx² + I²R / (m_bus·C_p)) + ((3Δt)²/2) · (dT/dt)_hysteresis

Where Δt = 0.640 s (tick duration).

If T_(t+3) > 315 K:
    HexagramManager flags early thermal alert
    Pre-stages dry ice sublimation chokes before thermal pulse arrives
    Proactively forces salt/dry-ice buffer flag high (state OR 0b000010)

6.4 THERMAL CNS MANAGER — PSEUDOCODE SPECIFICATION

```
class ThermalCNSManager:
    private currentState: uint6 = 0b000000
    private tickCounter: uint64 = 0
    private plenumPressureIntegral: float = 0.0
    private critEntryTick: uint64 = 0

    public handleSystemTick(sensors: ThermalSensorPayload): uint6 {
        tickCounter++

        // 1. High-frequency analog sub-tick (within 640 ms window)
        executeSubTickFeedback(sensors)

        // 2. Compute 3-tick forward lookahead
        predictedTemp = predictThermalHorizon(sensors)

        // 3. Evaluate state transitions
        targetState = evaluateHexagramState(currentState, sensors, predictedTemp)

        // 4. Execute structural state commit
        if (targetState != currentState) {
            executeStateTransition(currentState, targetState)
            currentState = targetState
        }

        // 5. Adjust plenum PI damping
        adjustPlenumDamping(sensors)

        return currentState
    }

    private predictThermalHorizon(s: ThermalSensorPayload): float {
        dt = 0.640
        heatInput = s.busCurrent² × 0.001  // I²R Watts
        spatialGradient = s.electrodeTemp[0] - s.electrodeTemp[last]
        return s.electrodeTemp[0] + (3×dt) × (spatialGradient + heatInput)
    }

    private evaluateHexagramState(curr, sens, predT): uint6 {
        // CRIT timeout guard
        if (curr in [0b110111, 0b111011] && sens.crit_ticks > 47) {
            return 0b111001  // LIMP_MODE
        }
        // Proactive pre-staging
        if (predT > 315.0 && curr in [0b111000, 0b110100]) {
            return curr | 0b000010  // Force buffer flag high
        }
        return curr
    }

    private adjustPlenumDamping(s: ThermalSensorPayload): void {
        P_target = 1.3
        error = P_target - s.plenumPressure
        plenumPressureIntegral = clamp(plenumPressureIntegral + error×0.640, -0.5, 0.5)
        heaterOutput = (1.2 × error) + (0.4 × plenumPressureIntegral)
        applyHeaterVoltage(max(0, heaterOutput))
    }
```

SENSOR PAYLOAD STRUCTURE:
- busCurrent: float (Amperes)
- electrodeTemp: float[16] (Spatial Kelvin grid)
- plenumPressure: float (Atmospheres)
- saltHydration: float (0.0–1.0)
- vesselSpeed: float (Knots)
- wallPressure: float (Hydrodynamic load, Atmospheres)

7.2 MAGNETIC SIGNATURE — THREE-STAGE FLUX-DAMPING CASCADE

Problem: 2.3 T Halbach array generates static magnetic dipole moment detectable by
airborne MAD (Magneto-Anomaly Detection) at 200–500 m range.

STAGE 1: Silicon Steel Flux Diverter
- Material: High-silicon electrical steel
- Thickness: 0.8 mm
- Saturation flux: B_sat ≈ 2.0 T
- Function: Captures bulk stray flux, steers it around thruster duct
- Result: Drops leakage field from 2.3 T to 0.5 T

STAGE 2: Non-Magnetic Isolation Gap
- Material: 1.5 mm aerated composite
- Function: Blocks mechanical vibration transmission to outer shield
- Prevents reintroduction of acoustic signature via structure-borne path

STAGE 3: Multi-Ply Mu-Metal Shield Array
- Material: Fully hydrogen-annealed Mu-metal (Ni-Fe alloy)
- Permeability: μ_r ≥ 80,000
- Construction: 3 × 0.5 mm plies, separated by non-magnetic insulation
- Post-manufacturing: Final hydrogen annealing after all cutting and bending
- Shielding effectiveness: SE_total ≈ 65 dB
  (SE_total = SE₁ + SE₂ + SE₃ + 20·log₁₀(2d/δ)·(n-1))
- Result: External field < 50 nT (Earth ambient ~50,000 nT)
- Classification: MAD-invisible at tactical depths

7.3 MAGNETIC SILENCING ALTERNATIVES (NOT SELECTED)

PHASE 1: POWER PLANT WAKE (0–30 seconds)
- Reactor/fuel cell: Minimum stable output (10% rated)
- 24 V control bus: Energized
- ThermalCNSManager: Instantiated, state = 000000
- Sensor arrays: Powered and validated
- Verification: Control voltage 24.0 ± 0.5 V, all telemetry valid,
  HexagramManager self-test checksum valid
- Abort: Any sensor fault → remain in Phase 1, flag maintenance

PHASE 2: SALT HYDRATE PRECHARGE (30–90 seconds)
- Low-current bus bar heating: 5 A resistive trace
- Target: Warm bellows from 280 K toward 305 K
- No electrode current. No dry ice sublimation.
- Verification: Salt temp > 300 K, no localized hot spots (ΔT < 5 K)
- Abort: Temperature gradient > 10 K → possible bellows rupture, halt

PHASE 3: DRY ICE PLENUM PRESSURIZATION (90–150 seconds)
- Dry ice magazine isolation valve: OPENED
- Sublimation heating elements: 10% duty cycle
- Target: Ramp plenum from 1.0 atm to 1.3 atm over 60 seconds
- Verification: Plenum pressure 1.3 ± 0.05 atm, CO₂ temp 194 ± 5 K,
  no pressure oscillation (indicates ice bridging)
- Abort: Pressure > 1.5 atm → overpressure, vent to bilge

PHASE 4: MAGNETIC FIELD RAMP (150–180 seconds)
- Halbach array: Permanent magnet — no electrical excitation needed
- Shield degaussing: Decaying AC current through coils
  - Frequency: 60 Hz
  - Amplitude: 50 A → 0 A over 30 seconds
- Verification: External magnetic field < 50 nT,
  shield hysteresis loop closed (no remanent magnetization)
- Abort: External field > 100 nT → repeat degauss cycle

PHASE 5: ELECTRODE PRIMING (180–240 seconds)
- Seawater intake valves: OPENED
- Electrode channel: FLOODED
- Low-voltage test pulse: 100 V, 10 A (continuity check)
- Choke membranes: Set to 0% aperture (closed)
- Verification: Electrode resistance 0.1–0.5 mΩ,
  no ground fault (isolation > 1 MΩ),
  seawater flow rate 0.5 m/s (idle circulation)
- Abort: Ground fault → isolate electrode, drain channel, flag maintenance

PHASE 6: STEALTH IDLE (240+ seconds)
- State transition: 000000 → 110100 (STEALTH)
- All 5 choke membranes: Opened to 15% aperture (2-knot stealth profile)
- CO₂ transpiration layer: ACTIVE (vessel speed = 0, dockside or hover)
- Salt hydrate buffer: ACTIVE (T_bus maintained at 305 K by passive phase change)
- Dry ice plenum: ACTIVE at minimum sublimation rate (maintain 1.3 atm)
- SiC-MOSFET gates: ARMED, current setpoint = 0 (ready but not conducting)
- Verification: Acoustic < 20 dB, magnetic < 50 nT, thermal ΔT < 0.5 K,
  HexagramManager state = 110100, no fault flags
- Operational readiness: Can transition to TRANSIT (111000) in < 5 seconds
  or LIMP_MODE (111001) in < 1 second
