SECTION 4: WHY THEY PAY — THE REAL INCENTIVES
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

4.1 SOVEREIGN AGENCIES (Highest Value Tier)

They pay because your system gives them:
• UNDERWATER STEALTH: Zero tonal components, < 50 nT magnetic, no cavitation
• STRATEGIC AUTONOMY: No mechanical parts = no supply chain vulnerability
• FLEET MODERNIZATION: Replace aging propulsion on existing platforms
• MULTI-PHYSICS INTEGRATION: One system handles thermal + magnetic + acoustic
• RESEARCH LEADERSHIP: First-mover advantage in MHD propulsion
• DETERRENCE: Invisible submarines change strategic calculus

Payment justification: National security budget line items; R&D appropriations;
procurement programs. The $1.1B–$3.2B price is justified by the cost of
existing submarine programs ($2–4B per hull) and the operational advantage
of true stealth.

4.2 DEFENSE PRIMES (Industrial Multiplier Tier)

They pay because:
• RESALE: They can mark up and sell to sovereign clients at 2–3× cost
• INTEGRATION LOCK-IN: Once integrated into a platform, switching costs are
  massive — decades of maintenance contracts
• PLATFORM CONTROL: Owning the propulsion system means owning the platform
• SUBCONTRACTOR ECOSYSTEM: They can build an entire supply chain around your tech
• LONG-TERM CONTRACTS: 20–30 year platform life = 20–30 years of service revenue

Payment justification: Program of Record budgets; platform development
contracts; foreign military sales. The $600M–$1.8B is a platform development
cost, amortized across 10–20 hulls.

4.3 DEEP-TECH FIRMS (Commercial Application Tier)

They pay because:
• REAL PROBLEM SOLVING: Your submodules solve concrete industrial problems
• UNIQUE COGNITIVE SUBSTRATE: HexagramManager is unlike anything on the market
• NOVEL THERMAL SYSTEMS: Salt hydrate + dry ice cascade has no commercial equivalent
• ELECTRICAL INNOVATION: SiC switching at 5 kV with acoustic silence is unique
• SCALABILITY: Submodule licenses let them buy what they need, not the whole system

Payment justification: Venture capital; corporate R&D budgets; product
development. The $380M–$900M is product development cost, recovered through
product sales.

SECTION 5: DEAL STRUCTURE & SOVEREIGNTY RETENTION
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

5.1 DEAL TYPE COMPARISON

| Type            | IP Control | Upfront $ | Recurring $ | Risk | Preferred For |
|-----------------|------------|-----------|-------------|------|---------------|
| Tech Sale       | Lost       | Highest   | None        | High | Sovereign only |
| Stack Lease     | Retained   | Low       | High        | Low  | All tiers |
| Submodule License| Retained  | Medium    | Medium      | Low  | Deep-tech |
| Royalty         | Retained   | None      | Passive     | None | Manufacturing |

5.2 SOVEREIGNTY RETENTION STRATEGY

RECOMMENDED: Stack leases + submodule licenses as primary revenue model.
Tech sales ONLY to trusted sovereign partners with:
• Non-compete clauses (cannot resell or reverse-engineer)
• Audit rights (verify compliance with license terms)
• Termination clauses (breach = immediate revocation)
• National security riders (export control compliance)

IP PROTECTION:
• Core algorithms (GhostSplat, HexagramManager) remain cloud-hosted
• Physical hardware can be sold; software is always leased
• WASM parsers and zero-trust security are trade secrets, never licensed
• Manufacturing specifications are shared; design authority is retained

5.3 PRICING TACTICS

• ANCHOR HIGH: Lead with sovereign tech sale ($3.2B) to establish value
• CONCEDE TO LEASE: When buyer balks, offer stack lease ($300M/yr) as "compromise"
• SUBMODULE GATEWAY: Offer one submodule cheap ($5M) to prove value, then upsell
• BUNDLE DISCOUNT: Full system = 70% of sum of submodule prices
• EXCLUSIVITY PREMIUM: 2× price for exclusive rights within a sector
• ROYALTY BACK-END: Lower upfront in exchange for 8% manufacturing royalty

SECTION 6: BUYER QUALIFICATION CRITERIA
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

6.1 SOVEREIGN AGENCY QUALIFICATION

Required:
• National security clearance (TS/SCI or equivalent)
• Program of Record with appropriated funding
• Technical evaluation team (multi-physics expertise)
• Export control compliance (ITAR/EAR)
• Long-term commitment (minimum 5-year lease)

Red flags:
• No cleared personnel
• Unfunded mandate
• Political instability (election cycle risk)
• History of IP theft or reverse engineering

6.2 DEFENSE PRIME QUALIFICATION

Required:
• Existing submarine or underwater platform program
• Proven integration track record
• Foreign military sales authorization
• Financial stability (credit rating BBB+ or higher)
• Non-compete agreement

Red flags:
• No platform program
• History of subcontractor disputes
• Pending litigation
• Recent merger/acquisition (integration risk)

6.3 DEEP-TECH FIRM QUALIFICATION

Required:
• Series C or later funding (or equivalent revenue)
• Technical team capable of integration
• Clear use case and ROI model
• Willingness to accept stack lease (not tech sale)
• Reference customers

Red flags:
• Pre-revenue startup
• No technical team
• Unrealistic timeline
• Intent to reverse engineer

SECTION 7: COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

7.1 DIRECT COMPETITORS

None. There is no commercial MHD propulsion system on the market.

Historical precedents:
• Yamato-1 (Japan, 1992): Prototype only, no commercialization
• DARPA PUMP-JET programs: Classified, not available for sale
• Russian Alfa-class: Liquid metal cooled reactors, not MHD

7.2 INDIRECT COMPETITORS

| Competitor | Approach | Weakness vs. POG2-MHD |
|------------|----------|----------------------|
| Traditional propellers | Mechanical | Cavitation, noise, wear |
| Pump jets | Mechanical + shroud | Still has moving parts, gearbox noise |
| AIP (Air Independent Propulsion) | Fuel cell + battery | Limited endurance, thermal signature |
| Nuclear reactors | Fission | Massive size, radiation, cost |
| Superconducting motors | HTS coils | Cryogenic complexity, acoustic noise |

POG2-MHD advantages:
• No moving parts (zero mechanical wear)
• No cryogenics (dry ice replaces LN₂/He)
• Multi-signature suppression (acoustic + magnetic + thermal)
• Deterministic control (640 ms tick, predictive safety)
• Modular architecture (submodules can be sold independently)
