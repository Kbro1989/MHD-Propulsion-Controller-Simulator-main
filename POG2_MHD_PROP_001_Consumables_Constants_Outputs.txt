SECTION 1: CONSUMABLES — Materials Depleted During Operation
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1.1 DRY ICE (Solid CO₂)
    Mass:              10,000 kg magazine (60 cartridges, 0.3m × 1.5m each)
    Phase change:      Sublimation at 194.7 K (-78.5°C)
    Enthalpy:          571 kJ/kg
    Density:           1,560 kg/m³
    Insulation:        50 mm aerogel blanket (λ = 0.015 W/m·K)
    Heat leak:         ~2 kW total (negligible vs. 118 kW active load)
    Control:           Resistive heating elements (electronic throttle)

    CONSUMPTION RATES:
    • IDLE (0 kts):           0 kg/hr
    • STEALTH (5 kts):        745 kg/hr (thermal) + 6,912 kg per 6-hour stealth (transpiration)
    • TRANSIT (15 kts):       745 kg/hr (thermal only)
    • LIMP MODE:              372 kg/hr (50% thermal load)
    • PURGE:                  Variable (flood + blast cycle)

1.2 SALT HYDRATE (Glauber's Salt Matrix)
    Mass:              279 kg bellows bed
    Composition:       Na₂SO₄·10H₂O + 6% cross-linked polyacrylamide + 5% borax
    Phase change:      305.4 K (32.4°C)
    Enthalpy:          254 kJ/kg
    Thermal capacity:  279 kg × 254 kJ/kg = 70,866 kJ
    Cycle life:        ≥ 5,000 complete hydration/dehydration cycles

    CONSUMPTION:       Cyclic (reversible). Not permanently depleted.
    Spike protection:  118 kW × 600 s = 70,800 kJ (10-minute full-load protection)

    BYPRODUCT:         139 kg distilled water of hydration (emergency reserve)

1.3 SEAWATER
    Source:            Unlimited (open-loop ocean intake)
    Conductivity:      5.0 S/m
    Density:           1,025 kg/m³
    Kinematic viscosity: 1.2 × 10⁻⁶ m²/s
    Surface tension:     0.072 N/m (vs. CO₂)

    CONSUMPTION:       Continuous flow through thruster channel
    • IDLE:            0.5 m/s circulation (minimal)
    • STEALTH:         2.57 m/s (5 knots)
    • TRANSIT:         7.72 m/s (15 knots)

SECTION 3: OUTPUTS — What the System Produces
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

3.1 PRIMARY OUTPUT: THRUST
    Target density:      10,000 N/m²
    Transit:             100% thrust (10,000 N/m²)
    Stealth:             20–35% thrust (2,000–3,500 N/m²)
    Limp Mode:           75% thrust retention @ 50% power (7,500 N/m²)

    Thrust vector:       Streamwise, symmetric (checkerboard shed preserves centerline)

3.2 ACOUSTIC SIGNATURE OUTPUT
    IDLE:                < 20 dB (ambient ocean floor)
    STEALTH:             35–45 dB @ 5 knots (broadband turbulent flow only)
    TRANSIT:             45–55 dB @ 15 knots

    Characteristics:
    • Zero tonal components (10 Hz – 2 kHz)
    • No blade rate, no gearbox mesh, no pump harmonics, no cryocooler cyclic noise
    • CO₂ microbubbles resonate at 6.5 kHz (above sonar band)
    • Classified as biological/geological ambient beyond 500 m

3.3 MAGNETIC SIGNATURE OUTPUT
    External field:      < 50 nT
    Shielding:           ~65 dB attenuation via 3-stage cascade
    MAD status:          Invisible at tactical depths

    Shield cascade:
    • Stage 1: 0.8 mm Si-steel flux diverter (2.3 T → 0.5 T)
    • Stage 2: 1.5 mm aerated composite isolation gap
    • Stage 3: 3× 0.5 mm mu-metal plies (0.5 T → < 50 nT)

3.4 THERMAL STATE OUTPUTS
    Bus bar temperature:     < 305 K (nominal)
    Plenum pressure:         1.3 ± 0.05 atm
    Salt hydration fraction: 0.0–1.0 (real-time monitored)
    Dry ice remaining:       kg (real-time gravimetric)
    Thermal lag status:      τ = 0.9375 s (natural low-pass filter)

3.5 TELEMETRY OUTPUTS

    Predicted temperature:   T_(t+3) in Kelvin (1.92 s horizon)
    Fault flags:             46 reserved fault/recovery states
    Sensor array:            6-channel payload (current, temp grid, pressure, hydration, speed, wall pressure)
    Event log:               D1 event sourcing (audit trail)

3.6 POWER RECOVERY OUTPUT
    CO₂ expander:            ~5 kW (thermal loop turbine)
    Carnot efficiency:         30.7% (194 K → 280 K)
    Exergy recovery:         Waste heat converted to shaft power
    Status:                  Optional integration (Phase 2)

SECTION 4: WASTE STREAMS & BYPRODUCTS
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

4.1 CO₂ EXHAUST
    Form:        Sublimated gas (194.7 K, 1.0–1.3 atm)
    Destination: Bilge vent or recompression plant
    Phase 2:     Supercritical CO₂ closed-loop (optional)
    Mass flow:   0.32 kg/s (stealth) to 0.995 kg/s (transit, if transpiration active)

4.2 CARBONATE SCALE
    Composition: NiCO₃ / CoCO₃ (from DSA electrode passivation)
    Formation:   During PURGE cycle (CO₂ flood, 0.3 atm overpressure)
    Dissolution: Sacrificial — dissolves in free-stream seawater during transit
    Function:    Secondary corrosion barrier during initial high-speed phase
    Lifetime:    Minutes to hours depending on seawater saturation state

4.3 ELECTROLYTIC GAS
    Composition: H₂ + O₂ (from seawater splitting at anode)
    Minimization: CO₂ transpiration blanket suppresses oxygen evolution reaction
    Collection:  Purge cycle captures residual gas
    Hazard:      Minimal (suppressed by design, vented safely)

4.4 THERMAL REJECTION
    Path:        118 kW @ transit → SiC backing plate → dry ice → salt → seawater
    Character:   Cascaded, not dumped. Each tier extracts exergy before rejection.
    Ultimate sink: Ambient seawater at 280 K (unlimited capacity)

4.5 DISTILLED WATER
    Source:      Salt hydrate dehydration (Na₂SO₄·10H₂O → Na₂SO₄ + 10H₂O)
    Quantity:    139 kg total (279 kg salt × 50% water fraction)
    Purity:      Distilled (no chloride, no dissolved oxygen)
    Uses:
    • Emergency fresh water reserve (139 liters)
    • Electrode rinse during purge cycle
    • Humidity control in CO₂ plenum (30–40% RH, prevents static discharge)

SECTION 5: STATE-TO-OUTPUT MAPPING
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

STATE        BINARY   OUTPUT SUMMARY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
IDLE         000000   Zero thrust. Zero signature. Zero consumption. System dormant.
                      All contactors open. Dry ice sealed. Salt at ambient.

STEALTH      110100   20–35% thrust. 35–45 dB acoustic. Transpiration active.
                      CO₂ micro-bubbles at 0.32 kg/(m²·s). Salt buffer armed.
                      Dry ice at minimum sublimation (maintain 1.3 atm).

TRANSIT      111000   100% thrust. 45–55 dB acoustic. No transpiration.
                      Full 118 kW thermal load on dry ice. Salt cyclic.
                      Electrodes bare (no gas blanket). Seawater full flow.

TR_SALT      111010   100% thrust + salt buffer active. Handles transient spikes.
                      Dry ice normal rate. Pre-staged for thermal surge.

TR_CRIT      111011   100% thrust + max dry ice + max salt. Thermal emergency.
                      47-tick countdown to LIMP_MODE. All buffers at maximum.

LIMP_MODE    111001   75% thrust @ 50% power. Checkerboard segment shed.
                      372 kg/hr dry ice (conservation). 5,435 A current.
                      Recovery possible if T_bus < 305 K and resources allow.

PURGE        001001   Zero thrust. CO₂ flood. Carbonate passivation.
                      Electrode regeneration cycle. 4-step: isolate → drain → blast → exhaust.

ST_CRIT      110111   Stealth thrust + max thermal emergency. Same 47-tick guard.
                      Less common than TR_CRIT (lower baseline thermal load).
